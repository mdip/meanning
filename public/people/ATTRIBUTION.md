# Historical figure attribution

The four image assets are cropped, resized reproductions of hand-coloured
costume plates from:

- Jacques Grasset de Saint-Sauveur and Sylvain Maréchal, *Costumes civils
  actuels de tous les peuples connus*, 1788, volumes 1–4.
- Source scans: Boston Public Library, Internet Archive identifiers
  [`costumescivilsac01mare`](https://archive.org/details/costumescivilsac01mare),
  [`costumescivilsac02mare`](https://archive.org/details/costumescivilsac02mare),
  [`costumescivilsac03mare`](https://archive.org/details/costumescivilsac03mare),
  and
  [`costumescivilsac04mare`](https://archive.org/details/costumescivilsac04mare).

The 1788 publication is in the public domain. The bundled crops are taken from the listed scans and converted into transparent
subject silhouettes. They are not reproduced from Manning covers.
